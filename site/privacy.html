Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

YouTube Video Scheduler does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything it saves stays in your browser.

What is stored

The extension uses your browser's local extension storage (storage.local) to keep:

  • The videos you schedule: video ID, title, link and the time you picked.
  • Videos whose reminder was missed, so you can reschedule them.
  • Your Shorts limit and reminder message.
  • Whether the dashboard is open.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What is sent over the network

Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. On a YouTube watch page, the extension reads the page you are already on to find the video's length, so it can show the runtime at your playback speed. Video thumbnails in the schedule are loaded from YouTube, as they would be on YouTube itself.

Permissions

  • youtube.com access: to add the + button, the scheduler, the dashboard, the runtime display and the Shorts limit.
  • storage: to save your schedule and settings in your browser.
  • alarms and notifications: to remind you when a scheduled video is due.
  • tabs and activeTab: to open the video when it's due, count Shorts per tab, and toggle the dashboard on the current tab.

Analytics and ads

There are none, in the extension or on this website.
