Questions

Is it free?

Yes. YouTube Video Scheduler is free, open source under the MIT license, and has no ads or paid tier.

What happens if my browser is closed when a video is due?

The reminder can't fire while the browser is closed. The next time you open YouTube, the video shows up in the missed videos table, where you can reschedule it or add it back.

How do I open my scheduled videos?

Press Ctrl+I (⌘I on Mac) on any YouTube page. You can change the shortcut in your browser's extension shortcut settings.

How is the runtime at playback speed calculated?

The video's length is divided by your playback speed. A 12:30 video at 2x shows 12:30 (06:15).

How does the Shorts limit work?

The extension counts the Shorts you open in a tab. When you reach your limit (10 by default), it shows your reminder message. Change both from the extension's popup.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers such as Edge and Brave through the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox through Firefox Add-ons.
